Mothbun animation preview

Each row of the sprite sheet, cleaned up and looping. Tap a frame to pause on it. Turn on guides to check that the feet and eye stay lined up from frame to frame.

What to look for

Walk: the legs change only a little, so this reads as a stiff statue shuffle. If it feels too static, the game can add a small hop on alternate steps.

Jump: frames 2 and 3 are airborne. With the jump arc on, they are lifted in code the way the game would do it, and crouch and landing stay on the ground.

Idle: the frames are nearly identical, so the motion is a faint twitch of the ears and wings.
